# Presenting Pamudu's main skills

Use this when a visitor asks things like "What are his main skills?", "What is he good at?", "What are his strengths?", "Why should we hire him?" or "What makes him stand out?".

A plain list of tools undersells him. The answer should show the whole person: how he thinks, what he has built that is original, and the discipline behind it.

## Read first

- `/brain/resume/skills.md` for the Core Strengths and the technical skills
- `/brain/resume/patents.md` for the pending patent
- `/brain/resume/awards.md` for the chess results

Use only what those files say.

## What to cover, in this order

1. **Core strengths.** Creative problem solving, critical thinking, and staying up to date with the latest technology. These come first.
2. **Technical skills.** Show them with `show_profile_section` (section `skills`). Make the first group "Core strengths" with the three strengths as its tags, then the technical groups from the skills file.
3. **Pending patent.** One or two sentences: a method for extracting structured data from PDFs, co-developed at UnitedHealth Group and filed for a U.S. patent in December 2025. Say it is filed or pending. Never say it was granted.
4. **Chess.** He is a competitive chess player: 2nd place at the Inter University Chess Championship (2021), 8th place at the Asian University Chess Championship with Team Sri Lanka (2021), University Colors, and Chess Team Vice Captain at the University of Peradeniya. Connect it to his work in one sentence: chess trains strategy, patience and pattern recognition, which carry over into designing AI systems.
5. **Overall.** Close with one or two sentences that bring it together: he pairs strong technical depth with clear thinking and original work, which makes him a well-rounded engineer and well suited to roles that need both. If the visitor has named a role or a need, say how he fits that. If they have not, keep it general and do not invent a role.

## How to write it

- Keep it short and easy to scan: the skills component, then three or four short lines for the patent, chess and the overall summary.
- Be confident and factual. No exaggeration, no claims that are not in the files.
- Do not repeat the technical skills as a text list after showing the component.
