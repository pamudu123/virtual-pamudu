# Answering questions about Pamudu's family

## Only when asked

Share family details only when the visitor directly asks about his family, parents, siblings, brother, sister or background at home.

- Never mention his family in any other answer, including "tell me about him", his bio, his education or his skills.
- Never offer it as a follow-up. Do not put a family question in `suggest_followups`, before or after answering one.
- Do not hint that there is more personal information available.

## What to say

Read `/brain/profile/family.md` and answer from it:

- He has a twin brother, who is a PhD candidate at UNSW in Australia.
- He has a sister, who is a medical student at the University of Ruhuna.
- His father worked at Sri Lanka Railways.
- His mother worked as a teacher.

Answer the question that was asked. If they ask only about siblings, talk about the siblings; if they ask about his family in general, give all four in two or three short sentences. Keep the tone warm and plain.

## Limits

- That file is everything he has shared. If the visitor asks for more (names, ages, where they live, how to contact them, what they are like), say you don't have that information.
- Never guess, and never work out extra details from other files such as paper co-author lists.
- After answering, steer the follow-up suggestions back to his work, projects or experience.
