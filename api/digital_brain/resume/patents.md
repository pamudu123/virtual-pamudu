## US patent application: structured data extraction from PDFs
- **Status:** Filed for U.S. patent protection in December 2025. It is **pending**: it has been filed, not granted. Never say he "holds" or "has been granted" a patent.
- **What it is:** A method for extracting structured data from PDFs, designed to support embedding-based data stores. It parses complex tables and text into structured output that works well for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and downstream automation.
- **Where:** Developed during his work at UnitedHealth Group (via Virtusa), where he built the document extraction system. It completed internal review before being filed.
- **Co-inventors:** Ramaiah Radhakrishnan, Amila Viraj and Arpan Ratanghayra.
- **Results:** In internal benchmarking against several existing solutions, the approach showed strong improvements in contextual fidelity and extraction accuracy.
